# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Termalogy to be a welcoming and harassment-free space for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Give actionable technical feedback.
- Keep discussions focused on the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment or personal attacks
- Hate speech or discrimination
- Trolling, doxxing, or intimidation
- Repeated bad-faith disruption

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or block contributors that violate this code.

## Reporting

Open a private security advisory or contact maintainers via repository contact channels.

## Attribution

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant (v2.1) principles.
